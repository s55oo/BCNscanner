What is this?

BCNscanner monitors the MMMonVHF 2 m beacon list, resolves each callsign to its DXCC country via cty.dat, and computes distance/bearing from your home locator. With optional hardware enabled it can also turn your antenna (rotator) and tune your radio (rigctld) straight from the beacon list or radar scope.

Data refreshes every 12 hours from mmmonvhf.de and country-files.com; bundled fallback copies are used when the internet is unreachable.

Getting started

  1. Set LISTENING_GRID (your Maidenhead locator) in the server's .env file and restart the stack.
  2. Reload the page — the header shows your grid, and every beacon gets distance + bearing.
  3. To control hardware, enable rotator/radio options in .env first (they stay hidden otherwise).

Header readouts

  • Rig frequency (left) — live VFO frequency from rigctld; shown as demo value 144.400 MHz until hardware reports.
  • Antenna azimuth (right) — live bearing from the rotator controller; demo value 180° until hardware reports.
  • Values turn amber italic when you commanded them manually but the controller has not confirmed yet.

Beacon table

Click any column header to sort (click again to reverse). Columns:

  • MHz — beacon frequency; click to tune (when tune is armed)
  • Call / DXCC Country / Cont — callsign, resolved country and continent
  • Locator — beacon's Maidenhead grid
  • Dist km / Brg° — great-circle distance and bearing from your grid; click bearing to turn the antenna (when roto is armed)
  • ERP W — effective radiated power
  • Sts — status badge (see below)
  • Comment — operator notes from the beacon list; hover a row for antenna/QTF details

Rows inside the current beam (±30° of reported azimuth) get a subtle green tint; the tuned beacon's row is highlighted blue.

Radar scope

  • Your station sits at the center (green dot, labeled with your grid); North is up.
  • Beacons are plotted by bearing/distance; dot colour follows status, size grows when tuned.
  • The shaded green cone is your antenna beam (±30° around the reported azimuth).
  • Range rings adapt automatically to the farthest visible beacon.
  • Hover a dot for call/DXCC/km/bearing; click it to turn the antenna (rotator must be armed).

Rotator behaviour

  • Rotation is azimuth-only: every command sends just the target bearing.
  • Elevation is always set to 001 (1°) on each command — required by elevation-capable controllers that ignore commands without an EL field.
  • Supported backends (configured in .env): PstRotator HTTP (/PstRotator.htm?az=…&el=001), PstRotator UDP (<AZIMUTH>/<ELEVATION>) and GS-232 over TCP.
  • Azimuth is polled from the controller every 2 s; amber italic readout = commanded, not yet confirmed.

Beacon status codes

O Operational · T Testing · P Proposed · X Off air · U Unknown

Troubleshooting

SymptomFix
"Rotator disabled in config"Set ROTATOR_ENABLED=yes plus host/port in .env, then docker compose up -d.
"Rotor off" toast on clickArm control first: click the roto button.
"Tune off" toast on clickArm control first: click the tune button.
Demo values 144.400 MHz / 180° never changeHardware endpoints disabled in config, or controller unreachable — check docker logs -f bcnscanner-api.
No distances/bearingsLISTENING_GRID missing/invalid in .env.
Stale beacon dataList auto-refreshes every 12 h; restart the API container to force a fresh download.
